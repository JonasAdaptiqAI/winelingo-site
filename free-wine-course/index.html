Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Five emails, no payment details, no account needed. At the end we tell you about the full course, and that's it.

Do I get a certificate?

No. It is a friendly introduction, not an accredited qualification. It is a good first step before a course like WSET Level 1.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.

Is there a Danish version?

Yes: Danish sign-ups get the Danish emails. See the Danish page.

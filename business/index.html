Correct

Chablis is Chardonnay from cool northern Burgundy, mostly made without new oak: crisp, dry and mineral, so it lifts the fish instead of covering it. Sauternes is sweet, and the other two are big reds.

Why it matters

The guest asks. Can your team answer?

Wine sells best when the person at the table can talk about it. But teams change every season, there is no sommelier on every shift, and training has to fit between services.

New faces every season

Seasonal and part-time staff start on a Friday. With Winelingo they can begin on the basics from day one, on their own phone.

No sommelier on every shift

The app explains any bottle on your shelf in plain words: how it tastes, its story, what to eat with it and how to serve it.

Training between services

A lesson takes about five minutes. No classroom, no binder, no course day to book.

Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your team. New partners start with a free trial period, so the team can try Winelingo before you decide anything. Write to us and we’ll make you an offer.

Which phones does it work on?

Winelingo is an iPhone app, and the whole course also runs in the browser, so staff with an Android phone or a computer can take it too, on the same account. The staff tests and your house test are web pages as well. Open the course in your browser

Can you tailor it to our wine list?

Yes. We build a staff test from your list: ten questions the way guests ask them, each pointing to a bottle you actually pour. It lives on its own web page, which is not listed on Google.

Is staff data private?

Each person has their own account, and their tasting journal is theirs. As the employer you don’t get access to anyone’s account. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

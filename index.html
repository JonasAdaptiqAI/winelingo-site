Why does Cabernet Sauvignon lead on the Left Bank?
Its cool clay holds water
Its warm gravel drains fast
Its chalk stores summer water
Its sea fogs slow ripening

Key fact: Warm, fast-draining gravel suits Cabernet Sauvignon, so it leads Left Bank blends.

Why it matters: That is why Médoc reds are firmer and more drying, and the best age for many years.

Remember: Gravel for Cabernet, clay for Merlot.

Why it tastes like this

Cabernet Sauvignon gives the firm, drying tannin and the blackcurrant; Merlot rounds it out with plum. The Médoc’s warm gravel helps late-ripening Cabernet get fully ripe, and time in oak barrels adds the cedar and vanilla.

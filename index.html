How it works

From “no idea” to “I’ll have this one”

No wine words to memorise first. Winelingo starts with the bottle in front of you and teaches from there.

01

Point your camera

Snap a bottle label or a whole restaurant wine list. That’s the entire input — no typing, no searching.

02

Meet the wine

Get the identity, a plain-language taste profile, why it tastes that way, an honest note on the winery, and a story to tell as you pour.

03

Learn a little daily

Rate what you drink and Winelingo maps your palate. A short daily lesson and a word of the day keep the habit — and your streak — alive.

Questions

Good to know

Is Winelingo free?

Yes. Winelingo launches completely free, with no in-app purchases — you get the full experience at launch. (We may add optional extras down the road, but the launch app is free to use.)

When can I get it?

Winelingo is coming to the App Store very soon. Join the waitlist above and we’ll email you the moment it’s available to download — that’s the fastest way to hear.

Which devices does it work on?

Winelingo is an iPhone app at launch. You’ll point your iPhone camera at a label or wine list to scan it.

What languages does it support?

English, Danish, Spanish and German — with the same warm, plain-language teaching in each.

Do I need an account?

Yes — your tasting journal and palate profile are personal to you, so Winelingo asks you to create an account to keep them safe and in sync. You can delete your account and all its data at any time, right inside the app.

What happens to the photos I scan?

A label or wine-list photo is processed to identify the wine and return your result — it isn’t kept as a long-term gallery, and it’s never used to build an advertising profile. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

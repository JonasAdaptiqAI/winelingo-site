Questions

Good to know

Is Winelingo free?

Winelingo is free to download, with Premium for unlimited scans. The daily lesson, the wine word of the day and the whole Explore library are free to learn with. Premium is for people who scan a lot, restaurant wine lists included.

What is Winelingo?

Winelingo is a free app to learn about wine on your iPhone. Scan any wine label and it explains the bottle in plain English — body, tannin, acidity and sweetness — and builds your palate profile as you go. It speaks English, Danish, Spanish and German.

Where can I get it?

Right now — Winelingo is live on the App Store, free to download. It’s an iPhone app, and it works in English, Dansk, Español and Deutsch. On a computer, scan the QR code on this page with your iPhone camera to go straight to it.

Which devices does it work on?

Winelingo is an iPhone app. You point your iPhone camera at a label or wine list to scan it.

What languages does it support?

English, Danish, Spanish and German — with the same warm, plain-language teaching in each.

Do I need an account?

Yes — your tasting journal and palate profile are personal to you, so Winelingo asks you to create an account to keep them safe and in sync. You can delete your account and all its data at any time, right inside the app.

What happens to the photos I scan?

A label or wine-list photo is processed to identify the wine and return your result — it isn’t kept as a long-term gallery, and it’s never used to build an advertising profile. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Questions & answers

How do I join the waitlist?

Head to the homepage and enter your email in the “Join the waitlist” box. We’ll email you the moment Winelingo is available on the App Store — nothing else.

Is Winelingo available yet?

Winelingo is coming to the App Store very soon. It launches free, with no in-app purchases. Join the waitlist to be first to know.

Which devices are supported?

Winelingo is an iPhone app at launch. You point your iPhone camera at a wine label or wine list to scan it.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, right inside the app, under Profile → Delete account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How is my data handled?

Your tasting journal and palate profile are private to you. Scanned photos are processed to return your result and aren’t kept as a gallery or used for advertising. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Winelingo vs Vivino

Vivino tells you what other people thought of a wine. Winelingo teaches you to understand it yourself. Many people will want both.

Last checked 10 November 2026

Who wrote this. Winelingo did, and Winelingo is one of the apps on this page, so read it with that in mind. Facts about other apps come from their App Store listings and their own websites, with the sources linked under each table, and were checked on 10 November 2026. We did not install and test every app. Found something wrong or out of date? Tell us and we will fix it.

The short answer

Choose Vivino if you want a crowd rating and reviews for the bottle in front of you, or want to buy wine in the app. It is far bigger: Vivino states over 70 million users and 16 million wines.

Choose Winelingo if you want to learn: why a wine tastes the way it does, how to read a label or a wine list, and what you like. It has lessons, games and practice tests, and it does not sell wine.

Where Vivino is the better choice

  • You want to know what thousands of other drinkers thought of a bottle
  • You want to order wine from the app
  • You have an Android phone and want a native app
  • You want a cellar list as well as a journal
  • You scan rare or unusual bottles: Vivino's database is far larger

Where Winelingo is the better choice

  • You want to learn wine step by step, a few minutes a day
  • You want the scan to explain the wine in plain words, not give a score
  • You want an app with no shop and no ads
  • You are preparing for a first wine course or exam
  • You train restaurant staff

What each app does with a scan

Both apps scan a label. Vivino shows a community rating, reviews, tasting notes, food pairings and where to buy the wine, and its listing describes a taste profile built from your ratings with a "Match for You" score. Winelingo shows a taste profile (body, tannin, acidity, sweetness), explains why the wine tastes that way from its grapes and region, suggests food, and offers a short lesson linked to the bottle.

Both also scan restaurant wine lists, in their paid tiers. Vivino lists its wine-list scanner as a Premium benefit: it shows the star rating for every wine on the list, and the App Store listing says it helps you spot the best bottles for your taste. Winelingo's list scan, also in Premium, points to the bottles that match the palate you have built from your own ratings.

Learning

This is the real difference. Vivino's App Store listing describes no lessons, courses or quizzes; it describes an app that helps you "discover, choose, and buy the right wine". Winelingo is a course: 178 lessons in 27 units, games, and free practice tests.

Price

Both are free to download. Vivino Premium is sold in the app; the US App Store lists Premium purchases from $4.99. Winelingo Premium is $3.99 a month, or the course alone is $39.99 once. See Winelingo's prices.

Can you use both?

Yes, and it is a sensible pairing: Vivino at the shop shelf for a quick second opinion, Winelingo for learning what the labels mean so that you need the second opinion less.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Winelingo better than Vivino?

Not better, different. Vivino is the stronger choice for crowd ratings, reviews and buying wine, and it has a far larger wine database and an Android app. Winelingo is the stronger choice for learning about wine: it has lessons, games and practice tests, which Vivino's listing does not describe.

Is Vivino free?

Vivino is free to download, and its App Store listing describes label scanning, ratings and reviews, a journal and a cellar without marking them as paid. Vivino Premium is a paid tier; Vivino lists the restaurant wine-list scanner among its benefits. The US App Store lists Premium purchases from $4.99.

Does Vivino teach you about wine?

Vivino's App Store listing does not describe lessons, courses or quizzes. It gives ratings, reviews, tasting notes from other users and food pairings for each wine. To learn wine step by step, use a learning app such as Winelingo or Wine Master.

Does Winelingo have ratings like Vivino?

Not in the same way. In Winelingo you rate wines for yourself, and the app uses your ratings to map your palate. A wine shows an average Winelingo rating once at least three people have rated it, but there are no public reviews, and far fewer ratings than Vivino has.

What the real exams look like

Before you pick a practice test, check that it matches the exam you will sit.

Sources: WSET Level 1 Award in Wines · WSET Level 2 Award in Wines · WSET Level 3 Award in Wines · Court of Master Sommeliers Introductory Sommelier Course & Examination (Americas). Checked 16 November 2026. Formats can change; the awarding body's current specification is the authority.

WSET's own web pages show different exam times in places; the figures above follow the specification PDFs.

At Level 1 and Level 2 the exam is multiple choice only, one mark per question, and nothing is subtracted for a wrong answer. Level 3 is different: besides 50 multiple-choice questions there are four short-written questions and a blind tasting of two wines, and you need 55% in each part. A multiple-choice mock prepares you for only one of those three parts.

  • Winelingo practice tests

    Website · free · no account

    Ours. A 50-question Level 2 mock exam, plus shorter sets for Level 1 (10 questions), Level 3 (30 multiple-choice questions) and the Introductory Sommelier exam (30 questions). Each has a timed exam mode and a practice mode that explains every answer. The limit: each exam has one fixed set of questions, so the second attempt shows the same ones. For more variety, use one of the larger banks below.

  • Sommo

    Website, iPhone, Android · web mocks free · no account

    Free browser mock tests for WSET Level 1, 2 and 3 with unlimited retakes, and a 70-question Introductory Sommelier practice exam drawn from a bank of 162. The Level 1 mock draws 50 questions from a bank of 180. Sommo's own page says its quiz is "not an official paper or a complete exam simulation". In the app, Levels 2 to 4 need the paid plan ($4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US), which also adds typed answers marked by AI.

  • portnwine

    Website · free · no sign-up shown

    Timed mock papers for Level 1 (30 questions in 45 minutes), Level 2 (50 in 60 minutes) and Level 3 (50 multiple-choice questions), drawn at random on each attempt, with a written explanation for every question at the end. The site does not say how large its question bank is or who writes the questions.

  • Eclavin

    Website, iPhone · web questions free · no account

    100 free questions each for Level 1 and Level 2 on the web, every one with a worked explanation. Level 3 is in the iPhone app only, behind a $17.99 unlock.

  • OpenExamPrep

    Website · free · no registration

    A general exam-prep site with 100 practice questions and explanations for Level 2 and 100 for Level 3. The site says its questions are "independently developed simulations" and that coverage and difficulty "may differ from the actual exam".

  • WSET's own specifications

    PDF · free · official

    The specification for each level has the exam format and five sample multiple-choice questions with an answer key; the Level 3 document adds one sample written question without a model answer. It is a small set, but these are the only official questions WSET publishes openly, as far as we found, and the document also lists every learning outcome you can be asked about.

If you are sitting Level 3

Most free resources, including ours, practise only the multiple-choice part of Level 3. The written part needs its own 55% and a different kind of practice: explaining why a wine tastes the way it does, in full sentences, from the vineyard to the glass. Of the resources here, Wine Exam Trainer (open questions marked by AI), Sommo (typed answers marked by AI, on its paid plan) and ThirtyFifty (short-written mock exams) describe practice for that part. Your course provider is the first place to ask for feedback on written answers.

For the Introductory Sommelier exam

The Court of Master Sommeliers Americas describes the Introductory exam as 70 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes with a 60% pass mark. Sommo has a free 70-question practice exam, Winelingo has 30 free questions, and Cork Dork and Brainscape have flashcards for it.

How to use a mock exam

  1. Read the specification first. It lists what can be asked. A practice test that covers something else is wasting your time.
  2. Sit one full mock against the clock early, to see the format and find your weak topics.
  3. Study the weak topics, then use a different set of questions. Repeating the same paper measures your memory of that paper.
  4. Answer every question. Nothing is subtracted for a wrong answer at Level 1 or Level 2.

No practice test replaces the course. To earn the qualification you must sit the exam through an approved WSET course provider.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Where can I find free WSET Level 2 practice questions?

Several sites offer them without payment. Sommo, portnwine and Winelingo each have a free 50-question Level 2 mock exam that opens in a browser with no sign-up shown. Eclavin and OpenExamPrep each have 100 free Level 2 questions with explanations. WineRevision says it has six Level 2 mock exams, free with an account. WSET's own Level 2 specification has five official sample questions.

Does WSET publish past exam papers?

No. Each WSET specification states that WSET does not release past papers. The specifications for Level 1, 2 and 3 each include five sample multiple-choice questions with an answer key, and the Level 3 specification adds one sample written question. Every other practice test is unofficial.

How many questions are in the WSET Level 2 exam?

The WSET Level 2 Award in Wines exam is 50 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, closed book. The pass mark is 55%; 70% is a pass with merit and 85% a pass with distinction. Each question is worth one mark and nothing is subtracted for a wrong answer. Source: the WSET Level 2 specification.

What is the WSET Level 1 exam format?

The WSET Level 1 Award in Wines exam is 30 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes, closed book, with a pass mark of 70%, according to the WSET Level 1 specification.

Are free WSET mock exams enough to pass Level 3?

Not on their own. The Level 3 exam has 50 multiple-choice questions, four short-written questions and a blind tasting of two wines, and you need 55% in each part. Most free mock exams practise only the multiple-choice part.

Is there a free practice test for the Introductory Sommelier exam?

Yes. Sommo has a free 70-question practice exam in the browser, and Winelingo has 30 free questions with explanations. Both are unofficial and not affiliated with the Court of Master Sommeliers.

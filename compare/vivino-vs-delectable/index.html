Vivino vs Delectable

Two label-scanning apps with different ideas: Vivino is crowd ratings and a shop, Delectable is a journal and a feed of wine professionals.

Last checked 10 November 2026

Who wrote this. Winelingo did, and Winelingo is one of the apps on this page, so read it with that in mind. Facts about other apps come from their App Store listings and their own websites, with the sources linked under each table, and were checked on 10 November 2026. We did not install and test every app. Found something wrong or out of date? Tell us and we will fix it.

The short answer

For most people in 2026, Vivino. It is actively updated (its iPhone app was updated in November 2026), has a far larger community, and has a cellar, food pairings and, in Premium, a wine-list scanner.

Delectable's strengths are its feed of sommeliers, winemakers and critics, unlimited free label scans, and Vinous reviews in its Premium tier. But its iPhone app was last updated on 14 September 2021 and its Android app in April 2024, so check that it works well on your phone before you move your notes into it.

Winelingo is neither of these: it is a learning app, and we make it. We have tried to write this page the way we would want a comparison of our own app written.

Ratings

Vivino shows a community rating out of five and written reviews for each wine. Delectable shows community ratings too, and adds Vinous critic reviews for Premium subscribers ($5.99 a month). If you trust critics more than crowds, that is Delectable's strongest point.

Buying wine

Vivino sells wine in the app through partner merchants; availability depends on your country. Delectable's listing also describes buying wine from your phone, but that listing dates from 2021, so we could not confirm it still works.

Journal and cellar

Both keep a record of what you scan. Vivino also has a cellar list. Delectable's listing describes no cellar tracking.

Price

Both are free to use. Vivino Premium is sold in the app from $4.99. Delectable's free version shows ads; removing them costs $1.99 a month, and Premium is $5.99 a month.

If you want to learn rather than rate

Neither app is built to teach: neither listing describes lessons or quizzes. For that, see the apps that teach you wine.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Vivino or Delectable better?

For most people Vivino, because it is actively updated and has a much larger community, a cellar and food pairings. Delectable's strengths are its feed of wine professionals and the Vinous critic reviews in its Premium tier, but its iPhone app was last updated in September 2021.

Is Delectable still updated?

Delectable's iPhone app was last updated on 14 September 2021 and its Android app on 15 April 2024, according to the App Store and Google Play. The website delectable.com is still online and is owned by Vinous.

Who owns Delectable?

Delectable is owned by the wine publication Vinous. Vinous founder Antonio Galloni acquired it in December 2016, as reported by App Developer Magazine at the time, and delectable.com carries a Vinous Group copyright notice.

Alternatives

Vivino alternatives, sorted by what you want instead

The right alternative depends on why you are looking. Here are six, each for a different reason.

Last checked 10 November 2026

Who wrote this. Winelingo did, and Winelingo is one of the apps on this page, so read it with that in mind. Facts about other apps come from their App Store listings and their own websites, with the sources linked under each table, and were checked on 10 November 2026. We did not install and test every app. Found something wrong or out of date? Tell us and we will fix it.

First: what do you want that Vivino does not give you?

Vivino is the largest wine app by its own figures (Vivino states over 70 million users and 16 million wines), and no other app here states a database that size. Here are six reasons people look for an alternative. Find yours.

  • "I want to learn about wine, not just see a score"

    Winelingo or Wine Master. Winelingo teaches in short lessons and games, and its scanner explains why a wine tastes the way it does; free to start, Premium $3.99 a month, iPhone plus any browser. Wine Master is a free course of more than 460 lessons on iPhone, Android and the web; its iPhone listing says the camera does not identify wines or scan labels.

  • "I mostly want prices and where to buy"

    Wine-Searcher. It compares prices across merchants and sends you to the merchant's own site to buy.

  • "I want a proper cellar"

    CellarTracker. Built for collectors: inventory, community tasting notes, values and drinking windows. The free tier tracks your bottles; a subscription ($40 to $500 a year by cellar size) adds prices and valuation.

  • "I want a journal of what I drink"

    Delectable keeps a photo journal and shows what wine professionals drink. Note that its iPhone app was last updated in September 2021. Vivino vs Delectable.

  • "I want an app that does not sell wine"

    Winelingo does not sell wine, and the listings for CellarTracker and Wine Master describe no wine sales; CellarTracker shows merchant prices from Wine-Searcher. Wine-Searcher does not sell wine itself either; it links to merchants.

  • "I want scanning, a cellar and lessons in one app"

    SomeWine, a new iPhone app (July 2026). The free plan is limited to 10 label scans in total and a 3-bottle cellar; Pro is $4.99 a month (somewine.app/pricing).

When to stay with Vivino

If what you want is a rating for an unfamiliar bottle in a shop, stay. Of the alternatives here, CellarTracker also has a large body of community ratings (it states over 13.6 million ratings and reviews), Delectable and Wine-Searcher have user ratings too, and Winelingo has very few. You can also keep Vivino and add a second app for the thing it does not do.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the best alternative to Vivino?

It depends on what you want instead. For learning about wine: Winelingo or Wine Master. For prices and where to buy: Wine-Searcher. For managing a cellar: CellarTracker. For a photo journal: Delectable, although its iPhone app was last updated in 2021.

Is there an app like Vivino that does not sell wine?

Yes. Winelingo does not sell wine, and the listings for CellarTracker and Wine Master describe no wine sales. Wine-Searcher compares merchant prices and links to the merchant but does not sell wine itself. None of them states a community as large as Vivino's.

Is there a free alternative to Vivino?

Vivino itself is free to download, and its listing describes scanning and ratings without marking them as paid. Of the alternatives, Wine-Searcher, CellarTracker, Delectable and Winelingo all have free tiers, and Wine Master's listing says it is entirely free.

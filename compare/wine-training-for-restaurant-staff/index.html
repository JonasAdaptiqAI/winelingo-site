The tools below do not all do the same job, so start with what your team is missing.

  • ShiftTrained

    Web app by text link · from $29 a month · free trial

    You upload the wine list as a PDF and AI writes the questions; staff answer on their phones from a text link with nothing to install. Every plan lists manager access, a leaderboard and score tracking. Plans go by number of employees: $29 a month for up to 20, $49 for up to 40, $99 for up to 99.

  • Tablo

    iPhone and Android app · $49.99 a month, unlimited staff · 7-day free trial

    Turns the imported menu, wine list included, into three-minute pre-shift quizzes, and shows the manager who has trained tonight and which categories are weakest. The plan covers 40 quizzes a month. It covers the whole menu, so wine is one part of it.

  • Service Well

    Phone web app · $15 to $25 per staff member a month · waitlist

    The manager enters the products by name (wine, cocktails, spirits, sake, beer, food) and staff get 60-second quizzes and flashcards. Managers see completion rates and knowledge gaps. Sign-up was by waitlist on the date we checked.

  • Winelingo for restaurants

    iPhone app and web course · price on request · free trial period

    Ours. We build one ten-question staff test from your wine list by hand, and the team also gets the full wine course and a weekly league. Where the three tools above are stronger: they build quizzes from your own list or products as often as you like (ShiftTrained rebuilds them when you upload the list again), they give the manager scores or completion data, and they publish their prices. Winelingo has no manager dashboard, and the employer cannot see anyone's account.

Reference tools for the floor

SommOne Server calls itself a staff education program built on your list, and lets a manager see how much each server uses it; Sorso lists staff training sessions on its Enterprise plan.

The WSET Level 1 Award in Wines is a beginner qualification with six hours of guided learning, including a multiple-choice exam, taught by independent course providers who set their own prices. One US school lists it from $289 per person. It gives each person a certificate, which matters to some staff and some venues. It does not cover your own list, and at a few hundred dollars per person it costs more than most of the tools above do in a first year, so it tends to suit key staff more than a whole seasonal team. If someone is preparing for it, there are free WSET practice tests.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • Is the gap the list or the basics? A quiz on your bottles does not teach why Chablis is crisp; a course does not teach which Chablis you pour.
  • Do you need to see who has done the training? If so, pick a tool with manager reporting. Winelingo does not have it.
  • How often does the list change? If it changes weekly, a tool that regenerates quizzes from the list saves work.
  • What phones does the team have? Check for Android as well as iPhone, or a version that runs in the browser.
  • How is it priced? Per venue, per employee band or per person. A per-person price adds up quickly with seasonal staff.

Two more products came up and are not compared here: WineStaff, whose site describes a private app for one restaurant group's own staff, and Oeno Academy, whose site said registration was not yet open.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the best app to train restaurant staff on wine?

It depends on the gap. For quizzes built on your own wine list with results the manager can see, look at ShiftTrained (from $29 a month), Tablo ($49.99 a month) or Service Well. For teaching wine itself in short lessons on staff's own phones, Knowbie and Winelingo do that; Winelingo also builds one staff test from the venue's wine list but has no manager dashboard. Typsy covers wine as part of broad hospitality video training.

How much does wine training for restaurant staff cost?

Published prices on 16 November 2026: ShiftTrained from $29 a month for up to 20 employees, Tablo $49.99 a month for unlimited staff, Service Well $15 to $25 per staff member a month, Typsy €120 per team member a year. Mitti's Wine Knowledge course is free. Knowbie, Lumintino, SommOne Server, Vinealto's business offer, Napa Valley Wine Academy's team courses and Winelingo give prices on request. A WSET Level 1 course costs a few hundred dollars per person, depending on the provider.

Can staff training be built on our own wine list?

Yes. ShiftTrained and Tablo generate quizzes from the wine list or menu you upload, and Service Well from products you enter. SommOne Server, Lumintino, Sorso and WineValueAI turn your list into a reference or a daily brief for staff. Winelingo builds one ten-question staff test from your list by hand, alongside its general wine course.

Do servers need a WSET qualification?

No, it is not required to serve wine. WSET Level 1 is a recognised beginner qualification that some venues pay for key staff to take. For a whole team, and especially for seasonal staff, a short course or quizzes on the house wine list are cheaper and quicker to start.

Is there free wine training for servers?

Yes. Mitti by SafetyCulture has a free Wine Knowledge course for servers that takes under 30 minutes. Winelingo has 13 free practice tests in the browser with no account, five of them written for floor staff (serving wine and guest questions among them), and the first lessons of its course are free. Vinealto's Foundation course is free.

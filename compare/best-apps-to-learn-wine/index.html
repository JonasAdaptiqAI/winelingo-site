  • Winelingo

    iPhone, and the course in any browser · free, Premium $3.99 a month

    Ours. 178 lessons in 27 units, from the basics through grapes, serving and twelve wine countries, with games, streaks and a weekly league. It also scans a label or a restaurant wine list and explains the wine, and has free practice tests. Where it is weaker: there is no Android app, it is new, and few people have rated it yet.

  • Wine Master: Learn Wine

    iPhone, Android, Web · free

    The listing describes more than 460 short lessons in 14 languages, with WSET Level 1 to 3 practice, and says the app is free with no ads or in-app purchases. If price is what matters, start here. Since version 1.6.0 (November 2026) it also has a label scan that reads the words on a label; its listing says the scan does not look the wine up in a database. First released in September 2026.

  • Sommy

    iPhone · two courses free, Premium listed at $8.99 and $49.99

    You pick a goal and follow a path of short lessons and quizzes; guided tastings start from a photo of the label and are saved to a journal. Wine 101 and Bubbles 101 are free from start to finish. Some courses are sold as separate packs. First released in August 2026.

  • Duo Vino

    iPhone, Android, Web · free daily quiz, Premium $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year

    A daily-habit course built around WSET Level 2: lessons by grape and region, flashcards, mock exams with a score per topic, and guided tastings. A good fit if Level 2 is the goal; less so if you want wine in general without an exam. First released in September 2026.

  • VINYO

    iPhone, Android · free to download

    Describes itself as "the Duolingo of wine": beginner lessons with quizzes, badges and challenges, a tasting journal and a social feed. Its listing also mentions discovering and shopping for wines from "diverse winemakers and local producers"; how buying works is not described. The App Store page lists no in-app purchases, while Google Play labels the app as having some, with no prices shown.

  • Winemind

    iPhone, Android · some courses free, $9.99 a month or $89.99 a year

    Short courses on tasting, grapes, food pairing, buying and serving, with quizzes that explain the answers. Its website lists nine courses with 60 lessons in total, in six languages.

  • True Wine

    iPhone · free, Premium $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year

    Game-style lessons and a map of wine regions. The app was last updated in March 2025.

  • Decanter Know Your Wine

    iPhone · over 10 free modules, then $19.99 a year or $0.99 a module

    Made by Feed Your Elephant with the wine magazine Decanter. It teaches with repeated questions spaced over time, so it suits memorising facts more than learning from zero. The app was last updated in September 2024.

  • Sommo

    iPhone, Android, Web · free tier, Pro (called Premium on its website) $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year

    The broadest of the five in this table: a label, menu and shelf scanner, a cellar, a tasting journal and WSET study from Level 1 to 4 in one app. The free tier has five scans in total and WSET Level 1 study. Where it is stronger than Winelingo: it has an Android app, a cellar and exam study up to Level 4, and more ratings (73 on the US App Store against very few for Winelingo).

  • Winelingo

    iPhone · 5 free label scans a month

    The scanner explains the wine in plain words (body, tannin, acidity, sweetness and why it tastes that way) and links it to the lessons. Wine-list scanning is part of Premium. It keeps a tasting journal but does not manage a cellar.

  • SomeWine

    iPhone · free, Pro $4.99 a month

    A 32-lesson course with label scanning, a cellar and food pairing. The free plan has 10 label scans in total and a 3-bottle cellar; wine-list scanning needs Pro.

  • Vinoh

    iPhone · core journal free, Plus listed at $4.99 and $44.99

    A wine journal first: a label photo starts an entry, and six guided "Wine Journeys" add short lessons and quizzes. English only.

  • WineBible

    iPhone, with a web companion · free download, Pro $1.99

    Lessons, quizzes, region guides and a food-pairing guide. Its App Store listing does not describe label scanning; its website mentions giving a label photo to an AI tasting coach. The app was last updated in April 2025.

  • Sensium

    iPhone, Android, Web · free drills, Premium $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year

    A trainer for blind tasting: a path through the most-planted grapes, drills on grapes that are easy to confuse, and a grape atlas. It is the only app here built solely around identifying grapes blind.

  • Brainscape

    iPhone, Android, Web · free to start, Pro about $8 a month when billed yearly

    A general flashcard app with ready-made wine decks for WSET Level 2, WSET Level 3 and the Introductory Sommelier exam.

  • Cork Dork Wine Study

    iPhone, Android · six categories free, Pro from $7.99 a month

    Flashcards, wine maps and map quizzes aimed at WSET Level 2 and 3 and Court of Master Sommeliers students.

  • Wine Trainer

    iPhone, Android · 48-hour free trial, then one-time purchases

    More than 4,700 flashcard questions with no subscription. On the App Store since 2011.

  • Wine Folly Plus

    iPhone, Android, Web · starts free, $99.99 for the full collection

    Maps and infographics from Wine Folly: a visual reference for study, not a course.

Is there a Duolingo for wine?

Several apps borrow the idea of short daily lessons with streaks and rewards. VINYO uses the phrase itself. Duo Vino is built on a daily habit with a mascot as tutor. Winelingo has streaks, hearts and a weekly league. Wine Master organises more than 460 lessons into "islands". Sommy has you pick a goal and follow a path of short stops, with daily streaks. None of them is made by or connected with Duolingo.

What differs is what happens outside the lesson. Sommy, Wine Master and Winelingo connect lessons to a bottle you scan; Duo Vino is built around the WSET Level 2 exam; VINYO adds a social feed.

How to choose

  • Check your phone first. Several of these are iPhone only, Winelingo's app included.
  • Decide whether an exam is the goal. If it is, pick an app built around that syllabus and add practice tests. If it is not, an exam app will feel like homework.
  • Try the free part first. Nearly every app here has one, though Wine Trainer's lasts 48 hours and two listings do not say what is free. The best app is the one you still open after a week.
  • Look at the last update. Three apps on this page have not been updated for more than a year.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the best app to learn about wine?

It depends on how you want to learn. For a step-by-step course in short daily lessons: Winelingo (iPhone and web, free to start, Premium $3.99 a month), Wine Master (iPhone, Android and web; its listing says it is entirely free) or Sommy (iPhone). To learn from the bottle in front of you: Winelingo, Sommo, SomeWine, Sommy or Wine Master, which combine lessons with a label scan. For WSET Level 2 study: Duo Vino, which is built around the exam, or Sommo and Wine Master, which include Level 2 practice. For blind tasting practice: Sensium.

Is there an app like Duolingo for wine?

Yes, several apps teach wine in short daily lessons with streaks and rewards. Winelingo has 178 lessons with streaks, hearts and a weekly league, plus a label scanner. Wine Master has more than 460 short lessons and is free. VINYO describes itself as the Duolingo of wine. Duo Vino and Sommy also use daily lessons. None of them is connected with Duolingo.

Which wine learning apps are free?

Wine Master's listing says it is entirely free, with no ads or in-app purchases. VINYO is free to download and its App Store page lists no purchases, though Google Play labels it as having in-app purchases. Most others have a free part and a paid part: Winelingo opens every lesson in the iPhone app for free with hearts that refill, Sommy has two full courses free, Sommo includes WSET Level 1 study, and Decanter Know Your Wine has more than ten free modules.

Which wine learning apps work on Android?

Ten of the seventeen: Brainscape, Cork Dork, Duo Vino, Sensium, Sommo, VINYO, Wine Folly Plus, Wine Master, Wine Trainer and Winemind. Winelingo has no Android app, but its course and practice tests run in the browser on an Android phone.

Can an app replace a wine course like WSET?

No. An app can teach you the same knowledge and help you practise, but only an approved course provider can give you a WSET qualification, and tasting with other people is hard to replace. Apps are a good way to start, to prepare before a course and to revise during one.

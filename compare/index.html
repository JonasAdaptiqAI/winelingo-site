Wine apps do different jobs. Pick by what you want to do, not by which one is biggest.

Start with the job

Most "best wine app" lists compare apps that are not trying to do the same thing. One helps you buy, one runs a cellar, one teaches. So the useful question is what you want the app for.

  • I want to learn about wine

    Winelingo (short lessons, games and a scanner that explains the bottle) or Wine Master (a free course with 460+ lessons). The learning apps compared.

  • I want to know if this bottle is any good

    Vivino: crowd ratings for millions of wines. Winelingo vs Vivino · Vivino alternatives · Vivino vs Delectable.

  • I want to find a bottle and a fair price

    Wine-Searcher: prices from tens of thousands of merchants.

  • I want to manage a cellar

    CellarTracker: inventory, values and drinking windows.

  • I am studying for WSET or a sommelier exam

    Cork Dork for flashcards, Wine Master or Winelingo's free practice tests for mock exams.

How we keep these pages honest

  • Facts about another app come from its own App Store or Google Play listing, its own website or Apple's public data. The one exception is who owns Delectable, which comes from news reports of the 2016 sale. Each comparison table links its sources.
  • In the tables, a dash for another app means its listing does not describe that feature. It does not prove the feature is missing.
  • Every page shows the date the facts were last checked. Apps change; if a page is out of date, tell us.
  • We did not install and test every app. We compare what each company says its app does.
  • Where another app is the better choice, the page says so.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the best wine app?

It depends on the job. To learn about wine: Winelingo or Wine Master. For a crowd rating on a bottle: Vivino. To find where to buy a bottle and compare prices: Wine-Searcher. To manage a cellar: CellarTracker. For exam flashcards: Cork Dork.

Is there an app like Duolingo for wine?

Yes, a few. Winelingo teaches wine in short daily lessons with streaks, hearts and a weekly league, and also scans labels and wine lists. Wine Master is a free course of more than 460 short lessons. True Wine and Decanter Know Your Wine also teach in short sessions.

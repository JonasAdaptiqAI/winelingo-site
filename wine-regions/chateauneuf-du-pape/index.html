A prestigious southern Rhône appellation famous for its rich, full-bodied red blends, typically led by Grenache.

Why Châteauneuf-du-Pape tastes the way it does

The hot, sunny Mediterranean climate ripens Grenache fully, giving high alcohol, ripe red-fruit flavors, and a generous, warming texture. Many vineyards are covered in large rounded stones (galets) that store the day’s heat and radiate it back at night, pushing ripeness even further. The tradition of blending up to 13 permitted grape varieties adds complexity, while Syrah and Mourvèdre lend color, structure, and savory, peppery depth.

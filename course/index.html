How the course works

Short, interactive lessons

Each lesson is a handful of illustrated cards and quick questions. A wrong answer comes back at the end, so it sticks.

Five minutes a day

Set a daily goal, keep a streak, and pick up where you left off on any device.

Practical from day one

Taste, read a label, order in a restaurant and pair food: the course starts with the bottle in front of you.

Web and iPhone, one account

Take it in your browser on any computer or phone, or in the Winelingo app. Your progress follows you.

Questions about the course

Is the wine course free?

The first two units, Foundations and Tasting & senses (17 lessons), are free on the web. The other 25 units are included with Premium ($3.99 a month, 3 days free) or the course for life ($39.99 once). In the iPhone app every lesson is open, with five hearts that refill over time.

How long does the course take?

About 22 hours in total. Lessons take five to ten minutes each, so most people do one or two a day.

Do I get a certificate?

No. Winelingo tracks your progress, streaks and test scores, but it is not an accredited qualification. It is a friendly way to learn the basics before a WSET course.

Do I need an iPhone?

No. The whole course runs in your browser on any computer, tablet or Android phone. The iPhone app uses the same account.

Which languages?

English, Danish, Spanish and German. You can switch at any time.

Can I use it to train staff?

Yes. Restaurants and wine bars already use it for their teams. See Winelingo for business.

German Wine Course

Riesling, the Mosel, the Rhine and the warm south. 7 lessons · about 51 min

Start with the free units See prices

The 7 lessons

  1. 1

    Germany at a glance: Riesling, rivers and a cool climate

    Why Germany's cool climate makes fresh, zesty wines, how steep river slopes help grapes ripen, its 13 regions, and Riesling, the star grape.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why Germany's cool climate makes fresh wines
    • describe how steep river slopes help grapes ripen
    • recognise trocken, Riesling and Spätburgunder on a wine list
    about 7 min
  2. 2

    The grapes of Germany: Riesling and beyond

    Riesling is Germany's star white grape and Spätburgunder its leading red. Most German labels name the one grape inside, so a few names unlock the list.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why German labels usually name one grape
    • describe Riesling and how Germany's top grape changed
    • decode Burgunder names and match grapes to styles
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    The Mosel: Riesling on steep slate

    Why the Mosel's steep slate slopes ripen Riesling in a cool valley, how its light, zesty, low-alcohol wines taste, and which bottles to look for.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why Mosel vines grow on steep slate slopes
    • describe the light, zesty style of Mosel Riesling
    • name famous Mosel slopes and pick a first bottle
    about 8 min
  4. 4

    The Rhine heartland: Rheingau, Rheinhessen and Nahe

    Why a bend in the Rhine makes Rheingau Riesling fuller, how huge Rheinhessen grew up, and why the small Nahe is a patchwork of rocks.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why the Rheingau's vineyards are sunny and sheltered
    • describe Rheinhessen's rise and its red-slope Riesling
    • explain why neighbouring Nahe wines can taste different
    about 7 min
  5. 5

    Pfalz, Baden and Franken: Germany's warmer south

    Germany's sunnier south makes riper, fuller wines: dry Riesling in the Pfalz, Pinot grapes in Baden and earthy Silvaner in Franken.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why Germany's south makes riper, fuller wines
    • match Pfalz, Baden and Franken to their signature grapes
    • spot a Franken wine by its grape and bottle
    about 7 min
  6. 6

    Reading a German label: from village to small print

    Read a real German bottle: ripeness versus sweetness words, village and vineyard names, the 2021 law, the VDP eagle, the back label and Sekt.

    You will be able to:

    • tell a German wine's ripeness from its sweetness
    • read a German village and vineyard name
    • decode a German front label, back label and Sekt
    about 7 min
  7. 7

    Choosing German wine: dry or sweet, and where

    Pick a German bottle with confidence: tell dry from sweet at the shelf, choose a region for the style you want, step up the ladder and find good Sekt.

    You will be able to:

    • spot a dry or sweet German bottle at the shelf
    • choose a German region for the style you want
    • pick a step-up bottle, a Sekt and a food match
    about 7 min

White Wine Grapes Course

From Chardonnay to Riesling, rosé and bubbles. 6 lessons · about 47 min

The 6 lessons

  1. 1

    Chardonnay: one grape, many styles

    Why one neutral grape gives crisp Chablis, rich oaked Meursault, buttery California and Champagne: how climate and winemaking shape Chardonnay.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why one neutral grape makes many styles
    • tell crisp Chablis from rich, buttery oaked Chardonnay
    • explain where butter, vanilla and toast come from
    about 8 min
  2. 2

    Sauvignon Blanc: crisp, green and zesty

    Sauvignon Blanc is a crisp white with mouth-watering acidity and strong smells of gooseberry, grapefruit and cut grass. It loves goat cheese, salads and seafood.

    You will be able to:

    • describe Sauvignon Blanc's acidity and classic aromas
    • tell Loire, Marlborough and Bordeaux styles apart
    • pick food that suits a crisp Sauvignon Blanc
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    Riesling: from bone dry to sweet, and how to tell

    Riesling is a high-acid white made at every sweetness level, so read the label: trocken means dry, while Kabinett and similar words describe ripeness.

    You will be able to:

    • describe how Riesling tastes young and with age
    • spot a dry Riesling on a German label
    • choose an off-dry Riesling for spicy food
    about 8 min
  4. 4

    Crisp white grapes to know and how to pick one

    What makes a white wine crisp, six crisp whites from Italy, France, Spain and Austria, and how to let the food choose the bottle.

    You will be able to:

    • explain what makes a white wine crisp and fresh
    • recognise six crisp whites and where they come from
    • pick a crisp white to match your food
    about 7 min
  5. 5

    Aromatic whites: perfume in a glass

    Gewürztraminer, Viognier, Muscat and Torrontés smell of flowers and fruit, but a sweet smell does not always mean a sweet wine.

    You will be able to:

    • recognise four aromatic white grapes by their scent
    • explain why a sweet smell does not mean sweetness
    • choose an aromatic white for spicy Asian dishes
    about 8 min
  6. 6

    Eight white grapes worth discovering, and their homes

    Link each white grape to its home: Chenin Blanc, Assyrtiko and Furmint are sharp and fresh, while Marsanne and Roussanne are rich and rounded.

    You will be able to:

    • link eight white grapes to their home regions
    • explain why some whites taste sharp and others rich
    • tell sharp, fresh whites from rich, rounded ones
    about 8 min

After Dinner: Dessert Wine, Port, Brandy and Coffee

Dessert wine, Port, brandy and coffee. 9 lessons · about 70 min

Start with the free units See prices

The 9 lessons

  1. 1

    Fortified wines: Port, sherry and Madeira

    What fortified means, how to tell the Port and sherry styles apart, a word on Madeira, and how to serve, pair and keep each one.

    You will be able to:

    • explain what makes a wine fortified
    • tell the main Port and sherry styles apart
    • serve, pair and store each fortified style
    about 8 min
  2. 2

    The Port ladder: from Ruby to Vintage

    Ruby, LBV, aged Tawny, Colheita and Vintage Port as one ladder: what each tastes like, how long it keeps open and how to offer one rung up.

    You will be able to:

    • explain the Port ladder from Ruby to Vintage
    • tell a filtered LBV from one that needs decanting
    • suggest one rung up by the glass without pushing
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    Dessert and sweet wines: how to pair them

    Pick a wine at least as sweet as your dessert, match light with light and rich with rich, and let fresh acidity keep it lively.

    You will be able to:

    • choose a wine at least as sweet as dessert
    • match classic desserts with the right sweet wine
    • explain why acidity keeps sweet wines fresh
    about 7 min
  4. 4

    Brandy and digestifs: Cognac, Armagnac, Calvados and more

    For new staff: what is on the after-dinner shelf, what VS, VSOP and XO promise, and one clear sentence for each brandy a guest asks about.

    You will be able to:

    • explain what VS, VSOP and XO promise on Cognac
    • tell Cognac, Armagnac, Calvados, grappa and Brandy de Jerez apart
    • answer a guest's quick question about whisky, rum or liqueurs
    about 8 min
  5. 5

    Whisky, rum and liqueurs: enough to answer a guest

    For new staff: one clear sentence for each whisky, rum, liqueur and herbal bitter a guest asks about after dinner, and how guests take their whisky.

    You will be able to:

    • explain single malt, blended Scotch, bourbon and rye simply
    • serve whisky neat, with water or on the rocks
    • describe rum, classic liqueurs and herbal bitters in one sentence
    about 8 min
  6. 6

    Coffee and after-dinner drinks: what goes with what

    Why sweet glasses and roasted coffee suit each other, what to pour beside an espresso, a cappuccino or the chocolates, and the pairs that clash.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why sweet drinks and roasted coffee suit each other
    • suggest a glass for espresso, cappuccino or latte
    • spot the after-dinner pairs that clash
    about 7 min
  7. 7

    Serving after-dinner drinks: Port, sherry and brandy

    For new staff: the right glass, temperature and measure for Port, sherry, dessert wine and brandy, how long open bottles keep, and what comes with the coffee.

    You will be able to:

    • choose the right glass for Port, sherry and brandy
    • serve after-dinner drinks at the right temperature and measure
    • judge how long an open bottle behind the bar keeps
    about 8 min
  8. 8

    Selling after dinner: dessert wine, coffee and a digestif

    The two moments to offer, a choice of two that sells without pushing, the right drink for each guest, and how to make yes, and no, easy.

    You will be able to:

    • spot the two moments to offer an after-dinner drink
    • offer a choice of two that sells without pushing
    • match dessert wine, Port or brandy to the guest
    about 8 min
  9. 9

    The after-dinner list: read it, price it, recommend from it

    How a dessert and digestif list is built, what each line and number means, how many pours a bottle gives, and how to suggest three drinks at three prices.

    You will be able to:

    • read any line on an after-dinner list
    • work out how many pours a bottle gives
    • recommend three after-dinner drinks at three prices
    about 8 min

How Wine Is Made: Winemaking Course

From grape to glass. 7 lessons · about 52 min

Start with the free units See prices

This unit is included with Premium ($3.99 a month, 3 days free) or the course for life ($39.99 once).

The 7 lessons

  1. 1

    How white wine is made, step by step

    From a gentle squeeze to a cool, slow change of sugar into alcohol: the steps that keep a white wine pale, fresh and fruity, from grape to bottle.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why white grapes are pressed before fermenting
    • describe the steps of white winemaking, press to bottle
    • explain why whites ferment cool and away from air
    about 7 min
  2. 2

    How red wine is made, step by step

    Red wine ferments warm with its skins, then is pressed and blended: follow every choice in the cellar, from the stems to the final mix.

    You will be able to:

    • walk a friend through red winemaking, stems to blend
    • explain why the cap of skins is worked daily
    • explain free-run wine, press wine and the assemblage
    about 7 min
  3. 3

    The grapes behind rosé and bubbles

    How rosé gets its pink from red grape skins, why rosé Champagne breaks the rule, and the grapes behind Champagne, Prosecco and Cava.

    You will be able to:

    • explain how rosé gets its pink colour
    • name the main grapes behind rosé and Champagne
    • tell Prosecco and Cava apart by their grapes
    about 8 min
  4. 4

    What oak barrels do: wood, air and time

    Why a barrel adds flavour, softens a wine and lets some of it escape, how size, age and forest change the effect, and why many fresh wines skip oak.

    You will be able to:

    • explain the three things an oak barrel does
    • tell how barrel size, age and oak type change flavour
    • spot when oak suits a wine and when it overwhelms
    about 7 min
  5. 5

    How sparkling wine gets its bubbles

    Bubbles are carbon dioxide trapped under pressure. Six methods trap it, from the bottle you buy to a tank or a gas cylinder, and each leaves its mark in the glass.

    You will be able to:

    • explain where the bubbles in sparkling wine come from
    • describe the traditional method from bottle to dosage
    • read the bubble-making method on a wine label
    about 7 min
  6. 6

    Sparkling on the list: Champagne, Crémant, Cava, Prosecco

    Read the sparkling section of any wine list: the four big names, NV or vintage, the sweetness words, and how to choose for the moment and the budget.

    You will be able to:

    • tell Champagne, Crémant, Cava and Prosecco apart on a list
    • read the sweetness words from Brut Nature to Demi-Sec
    • choose a sparkling wine for the moment and budget
    about 8 min
  7. 7

    How sweet wines are made: three moments to keep sugar

    Sweet wine keeps some of the grape's sugar: concentrated in the berry before fermentation, saved by stopping the yeast, or added back at the end.

    You will be able to:

    • sort any sweet wine by when its sugar was kept
    • explain why fortified wines are both strong and sweet
    • explain how drying, noble rot and freezing concentrate sugar
    about 8 min

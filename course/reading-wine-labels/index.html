How to Read a Wine Label: Course

Decode any wine label. 3 lessons · about 23 min

Start with the free units See prices

This unit is included with Premium ($3.99 a month, 3 days free) or the course for life ($39.99 once).

The 3 lessons

  1. 1

    Old World vs New World labels: place or grape?

    Why European labels name the place and New World labels name the grape, what each name promises, and how to find the grape a place name hides.

    You will be able to:

    • tell an Old World label from a New World one
    • explain why European labels name the place, not the grape
    • turn a European place name into its likely grape
    about 8 min
  2. 2

    Europe's quality ladders: one shape, four countries

    France, Italy, Spain and Germany rank wine on the same ladder of place, with two twists: Spain counts time in the cellar, Germany counts ripeness at harvest.

    You will be able to:

    • place AOC, DOCG, DOCa and Prädikatswein on one ladder
    • read Burgundy's rungs from Bourgogne up to Grand Cru
    • explain Spain's ageing words and Germany's ripeness levels
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    Label words: which ones are real promises?

    Some label words are set by law, like Estate Bottled, Spain's Reserva and Champagne's Brut. Others, like Old Vine, mean what the winery says. Learn to tell them apart.

    You will be able to:

    • tell rule words from marketing words on a label
    • explain what Estate Bottled and Reserve really promise
    • pick the driest sparkling wine from its sweetness word
    about 7 min

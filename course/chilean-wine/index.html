Chilean Wine Course

Carmenère, the Central Valley and the cool coast. 6 lessons · about 44 min

The 6 lessons

  1. 1

    Chile at a glance: Carménère, the Andes and the sea

    A thin country between the Andes and the Pacific, where cool sea air and cold mountain nights make clean, fruity wines, and Carménère is the signature red.

    You will be able to:

    • explain how the Andes and the Pacific cool Chile's vineyards
    • describe how Chile's natural walls kept out phylloxera
    • name Chile's own red grape and its most planted grape
    about 7 min
  2. 2

    The grapes of Chile: Carménère and more

    Chile's grapes came in two waves: Spain's País first, then Bordeaux's Cabernet and Carménère, with crisp coastal whites, fresh Pinot Noir and old vines reborn.

    You will be able to:

    • explain how Chile's grapes arrived in two waves
    • name Chile's key red and white grapes
    • match each Chilean grape to its typical style
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    The Central Valley: Maipo, Colchagua and Maule

    Chile's warm Central Valley makes ripe, rich reds: Cabernet in Maipo, Carménère and Syrah in Colchagua, and old-vine Carignan in Maule.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why the Central Valley makes ripe, rich reds
    • match Maipo, Colchagua and Maule to their best-known wines
    • describe Maule's old, dry-farmed bush vines
    about 7 min
  4. 4

    Cool Chile: the coast and the south

    The cold Pacific keeps Chile's coast cool for crisp whites and Pinot Noir, while the wetter south holds very old vines that small producers are now reviving.

    You will be able to:

    • explain how the Pacific cools Chile's coastal vineyards
    • match Casablanca, Leyda and Limarí to their clues
    • describe the south's old, dry-farmed bush vines
    about 6 min
  5. 5

    Reading a Chilean label: place, grape and Reserva

    A Chilean label shows place, grape and year, and its Reserva words mark extra alcohol, and sometimes wood, not years of ageing.

    You will be able to:

    • explain what Reserva and Gran Reserva really promise
    • follow Chile's D.O. boxes from region down to area
    • read a Chilean front label line by line
    about 8 min
  6. 6

    Choosing Chilean wine: Carménère, Cabernet and cool coast

    A buyer's guide to Chile: safe everyday reds, how to trade up by valley, the cool coast and old-vine south, the label words that help, and what to eat with each bottle.

    You will be able to:

    • choose a safe everyday Chilean red
    • trade up by valley, from Alto Maipo to Leyda
    • read Costa and Reserva on a Chilean label
    about 8 min

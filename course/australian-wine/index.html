Australian Wine Course

Shiraz, the Barossa and cool Australia. 6 lessons · about 44 min

Start with the free units See prices

This unit is included with Premium ($3.99 a month, 3 days free) or the course for life ($39.99 once).

The 6 lessons

  1. 1

    Australia at a glance: sunshine, Shiraz and the south

    Why Australia is famous for bold, ripe red wine from the Shiraz grape, why its vines keep to the south, away from the dry outback, and how a quarantine saved very old vines.

    You will be able to:

    • describe Australia's bold, ripe style and its signature grape
    • explain where Australia's vineyards are and what cools them
    • explain why some of the world's oldest vines grow here
    about 6 min
  2. 2

    The grapes of Australia: Shiraz and beyond

    Shiraz is Australia's signature red grape, rich in warm places and peppery in cool ones, and each other grape has a home, from Coonawarra Cabernet to Hunter Valley Sémillon.

    You will be able to:

    • describe how warm and cool Shiraz taste
    • match Australia's key red grapes to their homes
    • explain why Hunter Valley Sémillon changes so much with age
    about 8 min
  3. 3

    Warm South Australia: Barossa and McLaren Vale

    South Australia makes about half of Australia's wine. Its warm Barossa and McLaren Vale regions are famous for rich, ripe reds from Shiraz and Grenache.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why the Barossa ripens rich Shiraz and Grenache
    • explain what Old Vine means on a Barossa label
    • tell Barossa, Eden Valley and McLaren Vale apart
    about 7 min
  4. 4

    Cooler Australia: Yarra Valley, Tasmania and Margaret River

    Cooler, milder regions ripen grapes slowly, so Yarra Valley, Tasmania and Margaret River make fresher, more delicate wines, from Pinot Noir and sparkling wine to Cabernet.

    You will be able to:

    • explain why cool regions make fresher, more delicate wine
    • place Yarra Valley, Tasmania and Margaret River on a map
    • match Yarra, Tasmania and Margaret River to their wines
    about 8 min
  5. 5

    Reading an Australian label: the 85 per cent rule

    How to read an Australian label: the 85 per cent rule for grape, year and place, GIs from state to subregion, grape order, bin numbers and the back label.

    You will be able to:

    • explain what the 85 per cent rule covers
    • tell how precise the place on a label is
    • read an Australian front and back label
    about 8 min
  6. 6

    Choosing Australian wine: sunshine, Shiraz and more

    A practical guide to Australian wine: which region for which style, how the place on a label guides price, French classics' Australian cousins and easy food matches.

    You will be able to:

    • choose an Australian wine to suit the taste you want
    • match Australian regions to their speciality wines
    • use the price ladder and food matches when buying
    about 7 min
